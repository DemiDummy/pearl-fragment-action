# Pearl Fragment (action)

Wash up on a dormant living island, build a city on it, sail out, fight in real time and swallow pearl fragments for
their powers. A pirate action RPG in your browser, tier 1 of a bigger game.
**Play it at https://demidummy.github.io/pearl-fragment-action/**

Keyboard and mouse: W A S D to move, the mouse to aim, click to attack, Space to dodge, E to interact, Esc for the
menu and How to play.

This repo only holds the built game. It's published from a private source repo by `tools/release-pages.sh`.
